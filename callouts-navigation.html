Colour and typography

Two typefaces, one red, one indigo, and a family of greys — the whole visual language.

The palette

The brand red is loud by design, so it is rationed: course navigation, primary actions, and nothing else. Body links use the indigo of the global navigation — dark enough to pass contrast at body size, calm enough to live inside paragraphs. Everything structural is a grey.

Brand red · #E2231ACourse nav · primary buttons
Indigo · #2C236FGlobal nav · body links
Ink · #393D3EBody text · headings
Grey set#E3E5E8 · #C7CDD1 · #F5F5F5 · #F8F9FA

The typefaces

Lato carries headings, buttons and labels; Roboto carries everything learners read at length. Neither is ever bolded into a fake heading — if text needs a heading’s weight, it becomes a heading.

Quotes, two ways

The bordered quote is the workhorse — a red keyline, an italic voice, and always a credit. — A fictional practitioner

Overview cards

A landing page ends with two cards — one for first-time learners, one for returners — so neither audience reads the other’s instructions:

Start course

If this is your first time in this course, start here for the overview and the weekly schedule.

Modules

If you are a returning learner, go straight to the modules and continue where you left off.
