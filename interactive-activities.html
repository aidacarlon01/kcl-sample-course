The theme script

One small script, a short list of jobs — and a deliberate habit of moving jobs off it.

The global JavaScript does only what HTML cannot: it wires transcript toggles, and it opens glossary definitions in place. The accordion used to be on this list — Page 3 tells that story — and its removal is the point: every behaviour the platform can break is a behaviour worth rebuilding on something it cannot.

Glossary terms, defined in place

Courses define terms where learners meet them. Try the term in this sentence: a product’s north star metric anchors every roadmap conversation that follows.

What the script refuses to do

  • No layout — the stylesheet owns appearance, entirely.
  • No content — nothing learners read is injected at runtime.
  • No dependencies — it is a page of plain JavaScript, readable in one sitting.

That is the whole theme — four pages of components, each explaining itself.
